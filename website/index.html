Studio-Grade Processing

The Complete DSP & Effects Suite

A true audio engineering chain, not a toy equalizer. Twenty native stages, sixteen concurrent engines, live telemetry and honest conflict reporting — every stage independently bypassable.

10/32/64-Band Parametric EQ

Switch between a 10-band graphic curve and 32/64-band parametric engines. Eight biquad types, ±15 dB bands, per-band solo and mute, plus a 1024-node linear-phase FIR graphic EQ.

8 filter types ±15 dB FIR 1024

652 AutoEQ Headphone Profiles

A curated bundle of 652 five-band compensation curves, matched to the connected headphone by name. Search online for more, import your own, and stack a profile with room correction.

652 bundled Device match Import

Twenty-Stage Native Engine

One bit per stage, sixteen concurrent engines, and a lock-free render handoff. A bit-perfect fast path short-circuits the graph when the chain is clean.

20 stages 16 engines Lock-free

Dynamic EQ & Multiband Dynamics

Eight dynamic-EQ bands with threshold, ratio, attack and release. A four-band compressor uses Linkwitz-Riley crossovers, followed by a 4× oversampled true-peak lookahead limiter.

8 dyn bands 4-band comp True peak

Convolution Reverb + Custom IR

Nine programs from Studio 0.35 s to Cathedral 5.0 s, plus your own impulse responses up to two million samples / ten seconds. Partitioned FFT convolution with predelay, damping and cross-channel blend.

Crossfeed, Width & Saturation

Four crossfeed modes — BS2B, Chu Moy, Jan Meier and custom. A three-band imager with bass mono, plus Tape, Tube and Class-A saturation with 4× oversampling.

4 crossfeed modes 3-band imager 3 saturation models

Loudness, Sub-Crossover & Bass

A level-following loudness contour (+10 dB at 100 Hz, +4 dB at 8 kHz), Linkwitz-Riley sub crossover at 60–150 Hz with 12/24 dB slopes, and ViPER-style dynamic bass with nine presets.

ViPER-DDC & LiveProg Scripting

Load .vdc device-correction files with up to 256 sections and dual 44.1/48 kHz coefficient banks. LiveProg runs an EEL/JSFX subset: 8,192 instructions, 2,048 memory slots, eight sliders.

DAC Voicing & Channel Manager

Three reconstruction characters — Sharp linear-phase, Slow NOS-like and Minimum-phase — with 0–1 even-harmonic warmth. Manage 2–8 channels with per-channel trim and up to 50 ms delay at 768 kHz.

Room Correction Wizard

Measure with 24 log-spaced tones from 20 Hz to 16 kHz, capture a 48 kHz mono response, fit a ±15 dB correction curve with smoothing, stack it with AutoEQ, or export the result as FIR.

Dither, ReplayGain & Direct Volume

TPDF dither at a 16-bit boundary, ReplayGain track/album modes with preamp, and Direct Volume Control that keeps the Android stream at max while attenuating in the float DSP path.

Automix & BPM Analysis

Detect 40–240 BPM and one of 24 musical keys, build a 4/4 beat grid with four-bar phrases, and plan a transition. Analysis is cached for 500 tracks and survives restarts.

Per-Song Overrides & Profiles

Override EQ, volume and BPM per track, save DSP settings per device, and keep capture/apply snapshots of the whole chain with ten rolling backups.

Bit-Perfect & Hi-Res Output

DSD64–512, DoP, SACD ISO extraction, float output, AAudio direct and USB-DAC sample-rate negotiation. Lossy sources are blocked from a bit-perfect claim, and ambiguous containers report unverified.

DSP Inspector & 19-Channel Telemetry

See limiter, dynamic-EQ and multiband gain reduction, rolling RTF, thermal level, active stage mask and EQ headroom. A/B compare level-matches the dry path so loudness doesn't bias the verdict.

Stereo Imager & Platform Spatializer

Balance and mono panner, three-band stereo width with bass mono, and Android spatializer state surfaced honestly — no binaural claim we can't measure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Straight answers about the audio engine, Party, Quran Mode, AutoEQ and privacy.

Pulsr is an offline-first audiophile music player for Android, built with Flutter and a native C++ DSP engine. It combines a 20-stage DSP chain, bit-perfect USB-DAC output, 11 player themes, Pulsr Party group listening, an offline 114-surah Quran Mode, karaoke and a private Drift SQLite library. A separate Plus build optionally adds YouTube Music.

Pulsr bundles 652 five-band compensation profiles derived from published headphone measurements. It matches the connected headphone by name, applies the curve with a computed preamp, and lets you search online for more or import your own. You can stack a headphone profile with a separately measured room-correction curve.

Yes. Pulsr plays 14 file extensions including FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, DSF and DFF, and decodes DSD64 through DSD512 to PCM at up to 768 kHz. DoP carriers run at 176.4, 352.8 and 705.6 kHz where the DAC advertises them, and SACD ISO images can be indexed and extracted. A USB DAC can be driven exclusively at S16, S24_3 or S32, and AAudio offers a direct low-latency path.

Bit-perfect means the exact source bitstream reaches the DAC. Lossy codecs — MP3, AAC, Opus, Vorbis, WMA, AC-3 and live radio — have already discarded information, so Pulsr blocks them from a bit-perfect claim. Ambiguous containers such as .m4a or .ogg are allowed but report source_codec_unconfirmed, and 32-bit float sources are rejected rather than silently converted. Bluetooth is also blocked because it re-encodes.

The Pulsr Pure build removes the INTERNET permission at the manifest level and ships without Firebase, Sentry, cloud sync or YouTube code. There are no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers or accounts. Scanning, indexing, playback and playlist management happen on the device. Network features exist only in the optional Plus build and are off by default.

Each client exchanges timestamps with the Go party server over WebSockets and keeps the minimum-round-trip offset from a twelve-sample window. The server clock is monotonic, so NTP steps cannot move it backwards. A ten-second ping loop refreshes the estimate, and the client uses seek/watch deadbands instead of correcting on every packet. Rooms can be host-only or democratic, and the server is self-hostable.

All 114 surahs of the Tanzil Uthmani 1.1 text are bundled offline. Quran Mode adds five reciter-style presets, ayah repeat (single or range), Hifz blind recall, a Mushaf page reader, mosque ambience reverb and vocal warmth, tempo steps at 0.5×, 0.75× and 1.0×, and bookmarks that survive restarts.

Eleven: Clean, Classic, Card, Circle, Minimal, Vinyl, CD, Cassette, Waveform, Lyrics and Milkdrop. Each is a real player surface with its own layout and motion. Theme Studio adds custom palette, corner radius and ambient glow controls, with JSON export and import.

Pulsr reads embedded lyrics and sibling .lrc files, fetches missing lyrics from LRCLIB, and supports tap-to-seek lines plus a millisecond offset editor. Translation and romanization use online endpoints and target the app's current language. Karaoke mode adds YIN pitch tracking, a pitch highway, ±12 semitone shift and a 0–100 score per line.
