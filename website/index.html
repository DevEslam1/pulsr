AutoEQ is a curated open-source acoustic database of frequency response measurements. Pulsr embeds parametric compensation target curves (including Harman Target 2019/2018) for popular audiophile and wireless headphones (AirPods Pro, Sony XM5, Sennheiser HD600) to automatically flatten headphone peaks and restore natural acoustic balance.

Yes. Pulsr supports bit-perfect decoding of uncompressed and lossless formats including FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AAC, MP3, OPUS, and OGG Vorbis up to 24-bit / 192kHz sample rates. If connected to an external USB DAC, audio streams bypass Android's mixer where supported.

Absolutely. Pulsr contains zero advertising SDKs, zero analytics trackers, and zero network telemetry. It does not require any account creation. All metadata scanning, SQLite indexing, and playlist management happen strictly on your local device.

Pulsr automatically detects synced LRC files with the same filename in your song folder (e.g. song.lrc next to song.flac) as well as embedded ID3 SYLT/USLT metadata tags. You can also manually adjust lyrics sync with ±50ms latency tuning directly in the Now Playing screen.

Yes. Pulsr includes an in-place metadata tag editor that allows you to modify song titles, artists, albums, genres, years, and track numbers, as well as pick and embed high-resolution album cover artwork directly into audio files.
